# Five-minute usability task — prepared, not conducted

Status: **No external participant recruited; no actual foreign-resident user test completed.** Agent-operated browser checks are technical verification, not participant feedback. No participant count, quotation, success rate or satisfaction score is available.

## Participant instructions

Use only these fictional details; do not enter your own documents or identification numbers. Imagine you live in Seoul, have an E-7-1 status, moved to a rented home, and received a new job offer. Your address report has not been made. Find out what to prepare, mark one document ready, change your housing to employer-provided accommodation, and save the updated progress. Use the English interface on your preferred device. Explain what you expect when something is unclear.

## Observer record

Before starting, obtain consent to observe; do not record names, nationality, immigration records or raw personal notes. Record only device/browser, interface language, whether each task was completed independently or with help, confusing labels and optional participant comments in their actual words. Stop at five minutes. Do not coach before the participant attempts a step.

Tasks: enter situation → handle missing questions → find preparation/source → mark readiness → change housing → identify the added/removed documents → save progress. Finally ask the participant to explain the difference between owning, reusing and completing a document, and between a preparation plan and official permission.

Completion criterion for a future test: a real person attempts the flow and the observation is recorded. This protocol itself is not evidence of a test. Technical results are in verification.md.
